Common roofing website problems

Many contractor sites lose visitors before they ever ask for an estimate.

Hard to use on phones

Most homeowners search from a phone. Tiny text and slow pages push them away.

Unclear services

Visitors can’t tell whether you handle repairs, replacements or storm work.

Buried contact options

If requesting an estimate takes effort, many people won’t.

Little proof of quality

No project gallery or process explanation leaves visitors unsure.

Quick answers

How much does a roofing contractor website cost?

Our suggested starting prices are $799 (Foundation), $1,499 (Growth) and $2,499 (Authority). Final scope and price are confirmed in writing before work begins.

How long does a website project take?

It depends on page count, how quickly content and photos are supplied, and review rounds. We give a timeline estimate after discovery rather than a fixed promise.

What pages should a roofing website include?

At minimum: Home, Services (ideally one page per core service such as roof replacement and repair), Projects, About, and Contact or Request an Estimate.
