Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about working with us.

How much does a roofing contractor website cost?

Our suggested starting prices are $799 (Foundation), $1,499 (Growth) and $2,499 (Authority). Final scope and price are confirmed in writing before work begins.

How long does a website project take?

It depends on page count, how quickly content and photos are supplied, and review rounds. We give a timeline estimate after discovery rather than a fixed promise.

What pages should a roofing website include?

At minimum: Home, Services (ideally one page per core service such as roof replacement and repair), Projects, About, and Contact or Request an Estimate.

Will the website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every site is built responsive and checked on phone, tablet and desktop widths.

Can you redesign an existing roofing website?

Yes. See our website redesign service.

Can I request changes after launch?

Reasonable review and revision rounds are part of the project process. Changes after handover can be handled through an optional maintenance plan or a separate quote.

Is hosting included?

No. Hosting and domain registration are paid separately. Static sites can often be hosted inexpensively; we can help you set it up.

Will you help with website content?

We structure pages and can lightly edit supplied text. Extensive copywriting or professional photography is optional and quoted separately.

Can you add an estimate-request form?

Yes. We connect a form provider you choose. Form-provider fees, if any, are paid by you.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Optional maintenance is available, roughly $150–$500 per month depending on scope.

Can you help with local SEO?

We build on-page and technical SEO foundations. We do not offer guaranteed rankings, and ongoing SEO campaigns are a separate service.

What do I need to provide before work starts?

Your business name, services, service areas, logo (if any), project photos, licensing or insurance details you want shown, and a contact method for estimate requests.
