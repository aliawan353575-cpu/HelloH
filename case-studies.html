Demonstration Case Studies

These scenarios are hypothetical. They contain no real clients, traffic figures, conversions or revenue results.

Hypothetical demonstration

Summit Roofing Co.: outdated one-page site

Challenge: A hypothetical contractor has a single crowded page that is hard to use on phones.

Strategy: Split into focused service pages with a clear estimate path.

Design and development: Mobile-first layout, optimized images, lean code.

Features: Service pages, gallery, estimate form.

Intended UX improvements: Easier navigation and a clearer next step for visitors.

Hypothetical demonstration

RidgeLine Roofing: unclear inspection service

Challenge: A hypothetical repair company can’t explain what its inspections cover.

Strategy: Dedicated inspection page with a plain-language process.

Design and development: Step-based layout and FAQ accordion.

Features: Inspection page, FAQ, contact form.

Intended UX improvements: Visitors understand the service before they reach out.
