A Clear Process from Inquiry to Launch

Ten steps, so you always know what comes next.

  1. Initial inquiry

    You send project details through the contact form.

  2. Discovery and requirements

    We review services, goals, areas served and content.

  3. Sitemap and planning

    We agree the pages and structure.

  4. Visual design

    Layout, colors and typography for your brand.

  5. Development

    Responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

  6. Content and image integration

    Your text and photos are placed and optimized.

  7. Quality assurance

    Links, forms, mobile layout and accessibility basics checked.

  8. Client review and revisions

    You review and we refine within the agreed scope.

  9. Deployment and handover

    We launch the site and hand over files and instructions.

  10. Optional maintenance

    Ongoing updates under a separate plan.
